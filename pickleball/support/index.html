FAQ

How do I download the offline English voice?

Voice score calls need an offline English voice on your phone. On most Android phones: open Settings › System › Languages & input › Text-to-speech output (on Samsung: Settings › General management › Text-to-speech). Tap the gear next to the preferred engine (for example Speech Services by Google), then Install voice data › English (United States) and download it. Menu names vary by phone. Then return to the app; it checks again automatically, no restart needed.

Voice calls are silent. What can I check?

  • Voice calls are a Pro feature. Make sure Pro is unlocked and Voice score calls is on (setup screen, or the speaker icon during a match).
  • If you see "Offline English voice not found", download the offline English voice (see above). The app never uses network voices, so it stays silent until an offline voice is installed.
  • Turn up the media volume, and check that Do Not Disturb or silent mode isn't muting media.
  • Check that a Bluetooth speaker or headphones aren't connected somewhere else.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) linked to your Google account. On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same account and tap Restore purchase on the setup screen; Pro is also restored automatically when the app starts. Match history stays on the old phone and does not move with it. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

When do we switch sides?

Between games, teams change ends, and the other team serves first in the next game. In a single-game match or the deciding game (game 3 of best of 3, game 5 of best of 5), teams also change ends when one team first reaches 6 points (game to 11), 8 (to 15) or 11 (to 21); the server stays the same. The app shows a reminder with a countdown and swaps the teams on screen. Taps always score for the team shown on that side, so just tap the team that won the rally.

Why is the app landscape only?

The scoreboard uses very large numbers so they can be read from the court. It works in both landscape directions; rotating the phone between them keeps the teams in place. Portrait is not supported.
