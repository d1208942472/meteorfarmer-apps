Summary

We do not collect any data. The app has no ads, no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Your scores and match history stay on your phone.

Scores and match history

The current match and, with Pro, your match history (date, team names, game scores and rule settings) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. They are excluded from Android device backups (Google cloud backup) and from device-to-device transfer, so they are not copied off your phone or to a new phone. You can delete matches in the app; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Voice score calls

Voice score calls (Pro) are spoken by your phone's built-in offline speech engine. The app only uses a voice that runs on the device; it never uses a network voice, so the spoken score is not sent anywhere. If no offline English voice is installed, the app stays silent and asks you to download one in your phone's settings.

Internet use and purchases

The app uses the internet only for Google Play purchases. The optional one-time Pro purchase is sold and processed by Google through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. The Google Play Billing library included in the app communicates with Google Play to process and verify purchases. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising, tracking and accounts

The app contains no ads, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no accounts. We do not sell your data.

Deleting data

Delete matches in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date.
