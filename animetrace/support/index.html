Quick tips

  • AR mode: use good lighting and a sheet of paper with a visible edge. Move the phone slowly until the surface is detected, tap to place the image, then tap Lock Position.
  • Stand mode: prop your phone on a stand or a stack of books. Focus and exposure lock automatically after a moment for a steady view.
  • Use the sliders to adjust opacity and size.
