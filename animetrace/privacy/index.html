Summary

Anime Trace does not collect or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections of its own.

Camera

The app uses your camera only to show your drawing overlay on top of your paper, in AR mode or Stand mode. The camera feed is processed in real time on your device. It is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Photos

When you choose a photo to trace or turn into line art, it is processed only on your device. We never upload photos or send them anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small counter of how many tracings you have started, so it can apply the 3 free tracings. It also records whether the one-time unlock prompt has been shown. This information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising, tracking and third parties

The app contains no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
